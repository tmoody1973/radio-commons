Radio Milwaukee now works inside ChatGPT. It's in beta, and this is what it looks like.

Ask what's on any of our four stations and listen live, right in the chat. Ask what played at 7:45 this morning and save the song. Ask about a moment in an episode of This Bites and hear it from that second, in our hosts' own voices. See every restaurant from the episode on a map. Read this week's newsletter, find a concert by an artist our DJs play, or send the DJs a request.

The part I care about most: the answers come from Radio Milwaukee's own stories, playlists and events. Episode summaries are written from the episode and approved by our editors, and some shows are set to summary-only, because their stories deserve that care.

It runs on Radio Commons, the open-source server we built for Alexa+. One station server, two assistants.

Just over three-quarters of public radio donors rank local services above national programs (Greater Public). People are already asking AI assistants about their city. Local stations should be the ones answering.

We're opening it to a small group of listeners first. Public radio folks: if you want this for your station, let's talk.
