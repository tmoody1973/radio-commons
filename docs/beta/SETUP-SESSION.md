# Beta setup session (20 minutes)

A group call or an evening at the station where testers set up Radio Milwaukee in ChatGPT together. The goal is that everyone leaves with the app working and one piece of feedback already sent, and that the station learns where people get stuck.

## Before the session

- **Invite** with the date, the setup page link (https://radio-commons.vercel.app/beta) and three asks: bring a **computer** (not just a phone), know your **ChatGPT password**, and have your email open (for the Radio Milwaukee sign-up).
- **The morning of,** a staff member runs the whole setup on a fresh ChatGPT account. If any step on the page doesn't match the screen, fix the page before the session.
- **Roles:**
  - **Host:** shares their screen and walks the steps.
  - **Helper:** watches the chat and the room for anyone stuck, and helps one-on-one.
  - **Note-taker:** writes down every question and every place someone got stuck. This list is the most valuable output of the session.

## Run of show

| Time | What happens | Host says / does |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–3:00 | **Welcome** | What it is (the station inside ChatGPT), that it's a beta, and what we need from them: honest notes on what's confusing or broken. |
| 3:00–10:00 | **Set it up together** | Share the setup page and ChatGPT side by side. Do each step, then wait until everyone has done it. At the warning step, explain it: ChatGPT shows it for every plugin that isn't in its directory yet. |
| 10:00–17:00 | **Try it** | Everyone does #1 (open the station home) and #2 (listen live) together. Then free play with the rest of the list. Ask people to say out loud what they expected to happen. |
| 17:00–20:00 | **Send feedback, wrap up** | Everyone sends one piece of feedback from inside ChatGPT ("send feedback to Radio Milwaukee"). Say what happens next: a check-in email in two weeks. |

## Common snags (and what to say)

- **No ChatGPT account, or an account that can't add custom plugins:** they can watch and follow along on the host's screen; note it (we need to know how often this happens).
- **Only a phone:** they can't set it up there yet; pair them with someone, or follow up after.
- **The Radio Milwaukee sign-in asks for a code:** check their email; the code comes from Radio Milwaukee's sign-in service.
- **ChatGPT answers without the app:** start the message with "@Radio Milwaukee", or open a new chat.

## After the session

Within a day, send the note-taker's list to the team, and update the setup page wherever two or more people got stuck at the same step.
