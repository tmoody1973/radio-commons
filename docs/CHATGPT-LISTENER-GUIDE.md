# Radio Milwaukee in ChatGPT

**Your station, inside ChatGPT.** Ask about the song you just heard, the story you half-remember, or what's happening in Milwaukee tonight, and get the answer from Radio Milwaukee itself: our hosts, our reporters, our playlists and our event guide. Listen live, save songs, and find the places from our stories on a map, without leaving the chat.

*Status: **beta**. Radio Milwaukee in ChatGPT is new, and a group of listeners is trying it before it's listed in ChatGPT's app directory. Something broken or confusing? [Tell us](#tell-us-what-you-think).*

---

## Getting started

1. **During the beta:** Radio Milwaukee sends you a link and the steps to add the app in ChatGPT. **Later:** open **Apps** in ChatGPT, find **Radio Milwaukee**, and choose **Connect**.
2. Ask a question. To make sure ChatGPT uses Radio Milwaukee, start with **@Radio Milwaukee**, for example: *"@Radio Milwaukee what's playing on 88Nine?"*
3. Answers come with a card you can tap: play, save, open a map, add to your calendar.

You don't need an account to listen, find stories, or check events. Saving songs and following artists ask you to sign in once (see [Your account](#your-account)).

## What you can do

### The Radio Milwaukee home
*"Open Radio Milwaukee"* shows the station at a glance: what's on all four stations now, the newest episode of each show, this week's highlights, and your Finds. Tap one of the **Try asking** questions to get going.

### Listen live
- *"What's playing on 88Nine right now?"*
- *"What's on Radio Milwaukee?"* shows all four stations: **88Nine, HYFIN, Rhythm Lab and 414 Music**.

Tap **▶ Listen live** and the station plays right in the chat. While it plays, the card keeps up with what's on the air, about once a minute.

### Find that song
- *"What did 88Nine just play?"* or *"The last five songs on HYFIN"*
- *"What was playing on HYFIN around 10 this morning?"*
- *"What played on HYFIN between 10 and 10:30?"* lists every song in that window, newest first.
- *"When did you last play Nas?"* or *"Have you played the new Thao song?"* (searches about two weeks of plays)
- *"Tell me about that song"* gives the credits, album, year, and the artist's next local show.

Every song has a 30-second **Preview** and a **Save** button.

### Save songs to your Finds
Tap **Save** on any song, or say *"Save that song"*. It goes to your **88Nine Finds**, and to **Apple Music** too if you've connected it ([connect Apple Music](https://radio-commons.vercel.app/connect)). Ask *"What's in my Finds?"* any time. Songs you save by voice on Alexa show up here too, and the other way round.

### Our stories and podcasts
Radio Milwaukee's shows: **This Bites**, **Uniquely Milwaukee**, **Ladies First**, **Radio Milwaukee Artist Interviews**, **Milwaukee Music Premieres** and **Studio Milwaukee Sessions**.

- *"What was that This Bites episode about frugal dining?"* finds a story by what you remember, and for many episodes even by something someone said in it.
- *"What's new on Ladies First?"*
- Tap **▶ Play episode** to listen in the chat.
- Ask about a detail: *"What did they say about the stromboli?"* You get the answer in the episode's own words, with the moment it's heard ("At 10:45…") and a button to play from there.

Every story names its show and month, and summaries are Radio Milwaukee's own, written from the episode and approved by our editors.

### Places on a map
Food and community stories mention a lot of places. Ask *"What restaurants were discussed in the episode?"* or tap **Places** on a story card to see them **on a map**, numbered, with addresses. Tap **See all** for a full map you can pan and zoom, with directions and reservation links where available.

### Things to do in Milwaukee
- *"What's happening this weekend?"* or *"Any free events tonight?"*
- *"Any live music near El Tsunami?"* finds events near a place from one of our stories.
- *"What is Radio Milwaukee recommending?"* gives our staff picks and the weekly **MKE Concert Picks**.
- *"Which artists HYFIN plays have concerts coming up?"* lists shows by artists our DJs actually play, Milwaukee first.

Every event has **Add to calendar** and **Details**.

### This week at Radio Milwaukee
*"What's new at Radio Milwaukee this week?"* gives the highlights from our weekly newsletter, in our own words. Tap an item to play the episode, see the picks, or **Read** the article right in the chat: photo, headline and the opening, with **Read the whole article** for the rest and a link to radiomilwaukee.org. Ask follow-ups like *"Which of these are free?"* A few pages can't open in the chat yet; those open on our website instead.

### Shows and hosts
- *"Who's on 88Nine right now?"*
- *"When is Rhythm Lab on?"*
- *"Did I miss Audio Taste Test?"*

### Follow your artists
- *"Follow Tank and the Bangas"* (saving a song follows its artist too)
- *"What's new for me?"* tells you, since your last visit, how often our stations played the artists you follow, their upcoming shows, and our stories about them.
- *"Stop following Thao"*

### Send us a request
- *"Request No ID by Tank and the Bangas"*
- *"Suggest Johnny Cash's Hurt for 5 O'Clock Shadow"*, our daily 5 pm cover song: name the song, the original artist, and whose cover you'd like to hear.

ChatGPT asks what name the DJ should use (say "Tarik from Bay View", or skip it), then shows a card with **exactly** what we'll receive. Nothing is sent until you tap **Send to Radio Milwaukee**. Up to three requests a day. Requests need you to be signed in.

### Tell us what you think
Radio Milwaukee in ChatGPT is in beta, and your feedback shapes it.
- Say *"Send feedback to Radio Milwaukee"*, or tap **Send feedback** on the Radio Milwaukee home. Describe what happened in your own words; ChatGPT puts it on a card, and nothing is sent until you tap **Send**.
- Or email **digital@radiomilwaukee.org**. A screenshot helps.

Feedback needs you to be signed in, and counts toward the same three-a-day limit as requests. More help: [radio-commons.vercel.app/support](https://radio-commons.vercel.app/support).

## Your account

Saving songs, Finds, follows, requests, feedback and *"What's new for me?"* need a Radio Milwaukee listener account, so your saves belong to you. The first time, ChatGPT shows a **Sign in** screen; sign in (or create an account) and choose **Allow**. You only do this once.

It's the same account you'd use with Radio Milwaukee on Alexa, so your Finds and follows are the same in both places.

## Privacy

- We keep only what makes the features work: the songs you save, the artists you follow, when you last asked what's new, and, for 30 minutes, the last numbered list we showed you (so "save number 3" works).
- We know you by an account ID, not by your name or email. A request or feedback carries only what you see on its card before you tap Send, including the name you chose to give (or none). It's emailed to Radio Milwaukee's team.
- *"Delete my Finds"* erases your saved songs, your follows and your Apple Music link. Radio Milwaukee asks you to confirm first.
- Read the full policy: [radio-commons.vercel.app/privacy](https://radio-commons.vercel.app/privacy).

## Good to know

- **Answers come from Radio Milwaukee.** If we don't have a story or event, the app says so instead of guessing.
- **Memberships and donations aren't available in ChatGPT.** To support the station, visit [radiomilwaukee.org](https://radiomilwaukee.org).
- **If an answer looks out of date,** ask again or start a new chat. ChatGPT sometimes answers from an earlier conversation instead of asking Radio Milwaukee.
